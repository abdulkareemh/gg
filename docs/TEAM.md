# Noor AI — Team Structure

## Core Team (Phase 1 — MVP)

### CEO / Product Lead
- **Focus**: Vision, strategy, product-market fit
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Define product roadmap and priorities
  - Merchant interviews and onboarding
  - Investor relations and fundraising
  - Partnership with local business associations
  - Market research and competitive analysis

### CTO / AI Lead
- **Focus**: Architecture, AI agents, system design
- **Responsibilities**:
  - System architecture and tech decisions
  - AI agent design and Claude API integration
  - Code review and technical quality
  - Security and data privacy
  - Infrastructure and deployment

### Backend Engineer
- **Focus**: API, database, reliability
- **Responsibilities**:
  - FastAPI backend development
  - Database design and optimization
  - WhatsApp and Telegram API integrations
  - Payment provider integrations
  - Testing and CI/CD

### NLP Engineer
- **Focus**: Syrian Arabic language processing
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Intent detection and entity extraction
  - Syrian dialect vocabulary and patterns
  - Text normalization pipeline
  - Conversation flow design
  - Training data collection and annotation

### Growth / Operations Lead
- **Focus**: User acquisition, merchant success
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Merchant onboarding and training
  - Customer support and feedback collection
  - Social media and community management
  - Content creation (Arabic)
  - Metrics tracking and reporting

## Advisory (Phase 1)
- **Syrian Business Advisor**: Local market expertise, introductions
- **AI/NLP Advisor**: Arabic NLP research guidance
- **Fintech Advisor**: Payment integration strategy

## Hiring Plan (Phase 2 — Growth)
- Mobile Developer (dashboard app)
- Data Engineer
- Customer Success Manager (Arabic-speaking)
- Sales Representative (Damascus)
- Sales Representative (Aleppo)

## Culture Values
1. **User-first** (المستخدم أولا) — Every decision starts with the merchant
2. **Ship fast** (سرعة التنفيذ) — Weekly releases, rapid iteration
3. **Arabic-native** (عربي أصيل) — Product and communication in Arabic first
4. **Data-driven** (بالأرقام) — Measure everything, decide with data
5. **Resilient** (صامدين) — Build for unreliable networks, power outages, real conditions
