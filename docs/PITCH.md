# Noor AI — Investor Pitch

## One-Liner
WhatsApp-native AI assistant that helps Syrian small businesses manage orders, customers, and inventory — in Syrian Arabic.

## The Problem (حجم المشكلة)
- **500K+ SMEs** in Syria with zero digital tools
- Business owners manage everything via WhatsApp manually — orders get lost, no customer tracking
- Existing solutions (Shopify, etc.) are too expensive, English-only, require internet infrastructure
- **8M+ Syrian diaspora** want to send orders/gifts to family but have no digital channel

## The Solution (الحل)
**Noor AI** — plug-and-play AI chatbot for WhatsApp & Telegram that:
1. Takes and tracks orders automatically
2. Manages customer relationships (who orders what, VIP customers)
3. Tracks inventory with low-stock alerts
4. Processes payments via SyriaTel Cash / MTN Cash
5. Bridges the diaspora with local businesses

No app to download. No training needed. Just chat.

## Why Now?
- Syria entering reconstruction phase — businesses rebuilding
- Mobile money adoption accelerating (SyriaTel Cash, MTN Cash)
- WhatsApp penetration >80% in Syria
- AI/LLM costs dropped 90% in 2024-2025 — now viable for emerging markets
- No competitor focused on Syrian Arabic dialect
